This game was made mainly in WebAssembly Text Format. As in I handwrote the code. 

In this game you are a unicorn name Lumyora that is just trying to chill and have a little fun. You can play around as long as you like, but your main mission is to grab orbs that help Lumyora change colors and elements. Along the way, if you solve puzzles, you may gain hearts too... Are you able to solve all the puzzles and gain 5 hearts? The game ends when you touch the last orb (there will be one that is huge). 

Controls: WSAD J and K

May GOD bless and please enjoy the game!
